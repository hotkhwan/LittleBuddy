Little Days is a free game where young children care for a baby and learn their first English words by listening, touching and speaking.

How it plays

เล่นอย่างไร

Care for the baby

ดูแลน้องเบบี๋

The baby gets hungry, sleepy and playful. Your child feeds, bathes, dresses and tucks the baby in, room by room around a small house.

Hear the words, then say them

ฟังคำศัพท์ แล้วลองพูดตาม

Every action has an English word: milk, apple, bath, bed. The game says it first. Your child can tap to play, or say the word out loud. Both ways always work.

Collect stars

สะสมดาว

★ ★ ★ Stars mark what your child has done. There are no timers, no scores and no wrong answers, only "Great!" and "Try again!".

Made to play offline

เล่นได้โดยไม่ต้องต่ออินเทอร์เน็ต

No internet needed for the game

ตัวเกมไม่ต้องใช้อินเทอร์เน็ต

All rooms, words, pictures and voices are built into the app. Progress is saved on the device. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

No ads, no purchases in the app

ไม่มีโฆษณา ไม่มีการซื้อในแอป

The game is free. It shows no advertising and sells nothing. Grown-up settings sit behind a press-and-hold parental gate.

Meet Aliz

รู้จักอลิซ

Learn with Aliz, on the device

เรียนกับอลิซ ทำงานบนเครื่อง

Aliz runs short, scripted word lessons: a picture, a question, a cheer. The lessons ship inside the app and work without a connection.

AI Tutor planned

ครูสอน AI (แผนในอนาคต)

A conversational AI Tutor is planned for a later version. It would need an internet connection, a parent's permission first, and a small daily time allowance. It is switched off in the current build and cannot be turned on from the app.

For parents

สำหรับผู้ปกครอง

Parent Corner in the app

มุมผู้ปกครองในแอป

Behind a 3-second hold: Thai hints on or off, speech on or off, a speech diagnostic, and a reset that erases all progress from the device.

Family Club coming later

Family Club (เร็ว ๆ นี้)

A future family membership may extend AI Tutor time once that feature exists. There is nothing to buy today, and the app contains no purchase button.
