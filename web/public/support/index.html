Common questions

คำถามที่พบบ่อย

Is Little Days free?

Little Days ฟรีไหม

Yes. The game is free, shows no advertising and contains no purchases. A Family Club membership is planned for later; there is nothing to buy today.

Does it need the internet?

ต้องต่ออินเทอร์เน็ตไหม

No. The game plays fully offline. Only the planned AI Tutor would need a connection, and it is switched off in the current build.

Speech is not responding on iPad

ระบบเสียงบน iPad ไม่ตอบสนอง

  1. Open the iPad's Settings app, then Privacy & Security. Check that Little Days is allowed under both Microphone and Speech Recognition.
  2. In the game, open Parent Corner (press and hold for 3 seconds) and run the speech diagnostic. It names the reason speech is unavailable.
  3. Every activity also works by touch, so play can continue while you check.

Does speech work on Android?

ระบบเสียงใช้บน Android ได้ไหม

Not in the current build. The Android version has no microphone feature, so the speech option shows as unavailable and everything is played by touch.

How do I erase progress or start over?

จะลบความคืบหน้าหรือเริ่มใหม่ได้อย่างไร

Open Parent Corner (press and hold for 3 seconds) and choose Reset progress. This deletes the save file on the device. Deleting the app removes everything as well.

Can I turn Thai hints or speech off?

ปิดคำใบ้ภาษาไทยหรือปิดระบบเสียงได้ไหม

Yes. Both switches are in Parent Corner. With speech off, the game never listens and every activity is played by touch.

What does the game store about my child?

เกมเก็บข้อมูลอะไรเกี่ยวกับลูกบ้าง

Only stars, progress and settings, on the device. No name, no audio, no words your child said. The Privacy page lists everything.

Little Days is made for young children and their families. Game version 0.1.0.
