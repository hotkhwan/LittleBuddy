# Child playtest checklist — Little Days Founder Preview

For Aliz (Creative CEO / Child Playtest Director, ~7) with an adult beside her.
Run time: about 15 minutes. Print it or keep it on a phone.

## Before you start

- [ ] Device charged, **landscape**, volume up, Do Not Disturb on
- [ ] Fresh profile — Parent Corner → **Replay Mission 01** (this does **not**
      delete stars or settings)
- [ ] An adult is recording, or at least taking notes. **Do not coach her.**

The single most important rule: **say nothing unless she asks.** The moment you
explain a button, that button has failed and you will never know it.

---

## What to watch for (not what to ask)

Write down the *time* something happens, not just that it did. "Stuck 40 s at the
fridge" is actionable; "the fridge was confusing" is not.

### 1. The front door — 30 seconds
- [ ] Does she know which button starts the game **without being told**?
- [ ] Does she recognise Aliz? Does she say the name?
- [ ] Does she notice Bunny?
- [ ] Does she try to tap the characters? (She will. Does anything happen?)

### 2. Getting around — 2 minutes
- [ ] Does she find the joystick, or does she tap the floor?
- [ ] Does Aliz walk where she expects?
- [ ] Does she walk into walls / get stuck / fall out of the room?
- [ ] Does she find a door on her own?

### 3. Mission 01 — "I'm Hungry!" — 5 minutes
- [ ] Does she understand **Bunny is hungry** without the adult reading it aloud?
- [ ] Does she go to Bunny?
- [ ] Does she find the kitchen?
- [ ] Does she find the bottle?
- [ ] **Milk mini-game:** does she work out the pour-then-shake on her own?
      Note how long. If over ~30 s, note exactly what she tried instead.
- [ ] Does she carry the bottle back without being told?
- [ ] **Feeding:** does she hold the bottle at Bunny's mouth?
- [ ] Does she notice Bunny is happy afterwards?
- [ ] Does she notice the stars?

### 4. Mission 02 — "Snack Time" — 5 minutes
- [ ] Does she open the fridge on her own?
- [ ] Does she understand she is **carrying** something?
- [ ] Does she notice the banana became mashed banana?
- [ ] Does she get the food to Bunny?

### 5. Stopping — 2 minutes
- [ ] Close the app and reopen. Does it say **Continue**?
- [ ] Is her progress there?
- [ ] **Does she want to play again?** ← the only question that really matters

---

## Ask her afterwards (her words, verbatim)

Write down exactly what she says. Do not tidy it up.

1. What was your favourite bit?
2. Was there anything boring?
3. Was there anything you didn't understand?
4. What should Bunny be able to do next?
5. If you could add one thing, what would it be?

---

## Red flags — stop and write it down immediately

- [ ] She gets **stuck with no way forward** (the worst possible outcome)
- [ ] She taps something repeatedly and nothing happens
- [ ] She asks an adult what to do (note exactly where)
- [ ] She loses interest — **note the timestamp**
- [ ] Anything frightens or upsets her
- [ ] She tries something sensible that the game refuses

## Things we already know are unfinished — do not re-report

So the notes stay focused on new information:

- Aliz's hair is rough and her mouth is permanently open (replacement in progress)
- There is **no music or sound at all** yet
- Room signs are large
- The camera stays wide; it does not move in for close-ups
- Bunny's animation is limited
